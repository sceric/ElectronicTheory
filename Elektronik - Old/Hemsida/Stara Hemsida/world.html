Did you know there are over eight thousand artificial objects orbiting Earth? Over 2,500 are satellites, operative and inoperative. The remaining objects are orbital debris: parts such as nosecone shrouds, lens, hatch covers, rocket bodies, payloads that have disintegrated or exploded, and even objects that "escape" from manned spacecraft during operationsJava Tracker that tracks the Shuttle and Space Station uses orbit propagation. This site is run by the Public Affairs Office at the Johnson Space Center. (link:SATELLITE)
